# Medium canonical checklist

Every post on this site has an older copy on Medium. Until Medium points its canonical
at singhrishabh.com, Google may treat the Medium copy as the original and skip ours
("Discovered - currently not indexed").

## How to set it (per story, by hand)

1. Open the story on Medium → **⋯** → **Edit story**.
2. **⋯** (top right) → **More settings** → **Advanced Settings**.
3. Tick **"This story was originally published elsewhere"** and paste the singhrishabh.com URL below.
4. **Save** (the story must be re-published for the change to stick).
5. Check: view the Medium page source and confirm `<link rel="canonical" href="https://singhrishabh.com/blog/…/">`.

Medium may hide this option for stories in some publications (e.g. Stackademic). If it is missing,
ask the publication editor to set it, or move the story back to your own profile.
